Prompt lab

Use an AI helper without losing your own voice

These prompts work in any AI assistant your organization approves. The AI checks and organizes your findings. It does not examine the veteran, and it never invents a measurement.

Ground rules

  • You are the examiner. Every finding comes from your own exam and the records you reviewed.
  • No identifiers. Use only an AI tool your employer or practice has approved for health information. Otherwise paste de-identified notes.
  • Give it facts, ask for structure. Tell it to list missing details as questions for you.
  • Rewrite every rationale in your own words. Repeated wording across veterans looks like a template.
  • Read it against the form. Check each line against what you found before you sign.

Why wording matters now

In March 2026, VA said it would analyze roughly a million older DBQs, going back to about 2010, for fraud patterns. Reporting describes the signals as repeated boilerplate, unusually high volume from one provider, and internal inconsistencies. VA later said the tool would inform future detection and would not revisit finalized claims.

The practical lesson: document specific, individual findings that stay consistent from section to section.
