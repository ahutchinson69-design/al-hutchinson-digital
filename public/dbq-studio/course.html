How a report travels

Examiner, quality assurance, rater

Your AI is one check. A quality assurance reviewer, often with their own AI, is another. Neither replaces your own exam and your own review.

1ExaminerExamines, measures, writes the report, and signs it. May use AI to search the record and check the draft.
→
2Quality assuranceChecks completeness, consistency and support, often with their own AI. Catches what the first AI missed. Verifies against the report and record.
→
3RaterUses the finished report to decide the claim and the rating.

Program details vary by contractor and VA process. Learn how your own program handles QA, returns and addenda.
